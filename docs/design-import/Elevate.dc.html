Design notes
Deliberately unlike FleetSense: no slate/blue SaaS chrome, no white dashboard. Ink green + a single warm accent, one display face, 20px radii, dark surfaces reserved for the score and for anything the driver sees while the vehicle is moving.
Coaching copy states the behaviour and the fix, never a verdict on the driver. Wins are given the same visual weight as gaps.
Map, video and avatar art are placeholders.
